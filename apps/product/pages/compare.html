ON THE LIVE MAP

Nearby regions,
layered over the city.

On the live dashboard the comparison is geographic, not tabular. Open any city and the + Compare control lists the registered metros within 175 miles of it (haversine distance), sorted by display name. Select one or more and they layer onto the current city's map and signal panels, so you read neighboring metros against the same base map. The set you pick is carried through the dashboard's export flow; when a city has no registered neighbor within the radius, the control states that plainly instead of guessing. Try it from any city page — open the live dashboard — or pick a metro above and its compare menu reflects the same radius.

READING A COMPARISON

Differences are
declarations, not grades.

Metros differ because their registry entries declare different datasets — different portals, platforms, and cadences. A dash means the metro does not publish that feed family here; it is a statement about declared sources, not data quality. Coverage is stated per metro and never averaged away. The boundary section on /methodology applies to every column above.
