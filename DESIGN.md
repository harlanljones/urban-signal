# Urban Signal visual system

## Direction

Urban Signal is presented as a municipal field notebook: part city operations console, part annotated evidence board. The visual world borrows from transit diagrams, planning documents, and night-shift control rooms. It gives a technical product a point of view without turning the data into decoration.

The first surface uses a dark ink canvas with a paper interlude. The opening hex field is the signature: a spatial system that is alive enough to invite exploration, but precise enough to signal that every number has a source.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#0c1514`; elevated ink: `#12201e`; paper: `#eef2e8`.
- Lime signal: `#c5f36a`; coral warning/source gap: `#f2785c`; blue secondary signal: `#77b9e8`.
- Type: Manrope for interface and narrative; DM Mono for coordinates, labels, statuses, and measured values; Georgia italic for the human editorial emphasis.
- Rules are 1px and green-gray. Elevation comes from contrast and soft depth, not decorative shadows.
- The primary radius is 4px for tools and evidence surfaces; the signal core is circular because it represents a cell, not a generic card.

## Composition

The page moves from thesis → evidence trail → score composition → coverage truth → architecture → live dashboard. Dense systems are separated by quiet paper or ink passages. Repeated card grids are reserved for the city matrix, where repeated comparison is the task; the pipeline and architecture use buttons and columns instead.

## Interaction

The pipeline is a progressive disclosure control: each stage changes its detail panel and sample contract. The architecture explorer makes the system navigable by node. The city matrix filters without a page transition. Mobile collapses navigation and preserves the horizontal pipeline as a scrollable sequence. Reduced-motion preferences disable incidental movement and retain all information.

## Content rules

Describe feed availability exactly. A missing city source is a product fact, not a blank to hide. Keep model horizons at 6, 12, and 18 months, and keep the LIMS ingredients visible. Claims must point back to the repository, the city registry, or the live dashboard.
